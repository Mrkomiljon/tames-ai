# Response to reviewers — TAMES AI (revised submission)

Manuscript ID: [[ ]] · Title: TAMES AI: An Adaptive Fuzzy, Multimodal Ensemble Model for Malignancy Risk Assessment of Thyroid Nodules · Journal: [[ ]]

Dear [[Editor]],

We thank the editor and reviewers for a careful reading. We accept every point raised. The version we submitted contained internal editorial notes that should never have reached the journal, its Methods did not allow the work to be reproduced, and its reported results were not backed by a documented experiment. We have rewritten the manuscript around an experiment that is fully specified and reproducible from a public repository: the model is developed and tested on the public TN3K dataset with the official split, every equation is defined with a single notation, and all numbers in Section 5 are produced by the released code. Changes are marked in [[blue / tracked changes]]. Our responses follow.
